Student feedback

Selected Student Reflections

Students reflect on how the course shaped their understanding of macroeconomics.

“This class clarified a lot of my misunderstandings of macro by further dissecting each topic.”

“What I learned from this course is to look at the bigger picture.”

Selected excerpts from a course-feedback question included in the Summer 2022 final exam. With only four students enrolled, the question was included to encourage participation in course feedback. Students received full credit for this question for submitting any comment, regardless of its content or whether it was positive or critical; scores did not vary with the views expressed. These responses were not part of the anonymous course evaluation survey. Student names are omitted.
